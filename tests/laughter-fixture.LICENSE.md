# Laughter fixture

`laughter-fixture.wav` is the 20–24-second excerpt of **Laughter(s)** by **sagetyrtle**, converted to 16 kHz, mono, signed 16-bit PCM for a local acoustic classification check.

- Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Laughter(s).ogg
- Original: https://freesound.org/people/sagetyrtle/sounds/33658/
- License: CC0 1.0 Universal Public Domain Dedication, https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The fixture contains recorded laughter. It is not a recording of the user's microphone, browser or livestream.
