# VocaLume 4.5.1 — Japanese caption continuity

Date: 2026-10-25. Local hardware: Apple M5, 16 GiB, macOS 26.4.1. Model: HY-MT1.5 via the production desktop worker; speech recognition: MLX Whisper. This is a targeted regression check, not a blind accuracy evaluation.

## Changes and boundaries

- Recover a preceding recognized Japanese clause only when its grammar connects to the next fragment. The recovered words become visible source text and enter the normal translation/cache path; generated Chinese and unrelated conversation history are not used to invent missing Japanese.
- Keep at most the previous clause, capped at 120 characters, without accumulating repeated expanded captions. Check capture adjacency, known speaker changes, languages, reset and glossary changes. A matching suffix under a different prior clause has a different translation cache key.
- Join a bare polite verb ending only when capture explicitly ended at a limit and the next window begins at the exact same sample position. Validate all capture positions against the PCM duration. Older clients retain the stricter grammar-only behavior.
- Separate an auxiliary ending from a following unpunctuated lexical clause after joining; preserve connective endings such as `いませんが`.
- Extend a recent recognized unfinished clause beyond the usual eight-second audio limit, up to twelve seconds. Stale hints do not prolong fresh audio. Ordinary short replies keep the existing timing. The extra wait is an accuracy/latency tradeoff, not a universal speedup.
- Preserve the hesitant open ending of `それはちょっと…`.

The new caption can repeat the necessary preceding clause so that the completed meaning, including a late negation, is visible. Previously displayed history is retained. This does not retroactively guarantee that an earlier forced-cut partial translation was correct.

## Actual model checks

The frozen [37-case manifest](../../tests/fixtures/ja-caption-context-4.5.1.json) contains paired short/full Japanese inputs, ordinary short replies, adjacent continuation cases, an unpunctuated captured failure, explicit capture-cut verb endings and two Chinese-to-Japanese regressions. The first 28 inputs were measured before implementation; additional guards were added during development. These are authored diagnostic examples, not unseen holdout accuracy data.

[4.5.0 baseline, 28 outputs](4.5.1-context-before.jsonl). [Final 4.5.1 outputs, 37 cases](4.5.1-context-after.jsonl). Each result preserves its original and resolved model input. All final requests returned non-empty output; semantic findings were inspected separately rather than treating that as correctness.

Examples:

| Captured continuation | Before (fragment alone) | After (recognized prior clause recovered) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `魚が逃げて / しまいました。来てくれてありがとうございます。` | 就這樣結束了。非常感謝大家的參與。 | 魚兒逃走了。謝謝你們的到來。 |
| `勝てるかも / しれません。` | 不清楚啊。 | 或許可以勝利吧。 |
| `今日は時間がないので / 短めにします。` | 就寫短一些吧。 | 今天時間有限，所以會講得比較簡短。 |
| `それはちょっと。` | 那個有點誇張了。 | 那個有點…… |

The exact unpunctuated failure from 4.5.0, `しまいました来てくれてありがとうございます`, now resolves to `魚が逃げてしまいました。来てくれてありがとうございます` and returns `魚兒逃走了。謝謝你們的到來。` instead of inventing an ending or time reference. Negation and time survived the two ZH→JA regression inputs. Some outputs still have awkward phrasing or nuanced additions: the complete `明日はやります` example contains “會試著做一下”, and a context-free `明日は？` can still acquire an overly specific question. The feature addresses recoverable capture seams, not every ambiguous utterance.

## Limits

This cannot recover audio that was never received or repair every ASR mistake, missing word or ambiguous referent. A very long uninterrupted sentence still reaches the safety ceiling. A known speaker change blocks reference reuse; undetected speaker changes remain a limitation. No new physical Windows/Intel-Mac or microphone/accent matrix was run. The public preview download remains 4.4.5; 4.5.1 is the updated source and local installation, not a newly notarized public installer release.
