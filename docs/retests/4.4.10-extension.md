# Extension 4.4.10 — shorter caption cues

Date: 2026-10-25. Desktop source and the installed application remain 4.4.8. Public preview installer assets remain 4.4.5; this is an extension source/install update.

## Problem and change

Desktop capture previously accepted up to 20 seconds per audio window. Under load, waiting windows could be concatenated under the newest ID, leaving an earlier source-only caption without its final event. Four-entry display also grouped multiple windows by the entire acoustic utterance, creating long paragraphs.

The extension now looks for a voice-detection pause of at least 160 ms after four seconds of audio, with an eight-second hard ceiling. Early breathing pauses and recognized hanging clauses retain the existing longer pause guard. Each cue keeps its audio, ID and display row. Late recognition hints are scoped to the cue they describe. Original audio/text is not truncated to shorten the overlay, and the desktop translator still receives preceding translation context.

Waiting and failed captions have distinct display states. Failures remain retryable in recorded captions, and a valid final arriving after decoder recovery can repair a failed cue. Single-pair mode retains its translation-before-advance behavior.

## Limits

The eight-second ceiling is an audio segmentation limit, **not** a promised end-to-end subtitle latency. Inference, queued work and thermal throttling still add time. Continuous speech without a suitable pause can still be split within a sentence. This change does not establish YouTube-level latency or accuracy, or universal language/accent performance. The short synthetic fixture is a functional check, not a long livestream benchmark or a controlled before/after speed comparison. No new desktop build or signing change was required.
