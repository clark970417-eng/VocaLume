# VocaLume 4.5.9 — speech boundaries without extra capture waiting

2026-10-26. Baseline: `1dfeb103ed4148f9e5657da246d1bcc436126c24` (4.5.8). Apple M5, 16 GiB, macOS 26.4.1; existing MLX Whisper and HY-MT1.5-1.8B Q4_K_M. [Inputs, outputs and measurements](4.5.9-measurements.json).

## Change

Browser tab audio sent to the desktop translator could keep a stale unfinished hypothesis such as `今日は一緒に` after the speaker had already said its predicate. That stale flag vetoed a later breath, accumulating a long caption until a forced cut, sometimes inside the next clause.

- An unfinished ending protects the audio it describes and up to 384 ms of newly voiced continuation. More newly spoken audio expires that old veto; silence alone does not. A fresh `魚が逃げて` still gets its continuation safeguard. Delayed older feedback cannot overwrite a newer safeguard.
- A recognizable completed Japanese predicate can use an actual acoustic pause even when ASR omits the full stop. Text alone never forces a cut.
- After 2.5 seconds of cue context, a 320 ms detected breath can close a useful fragment. The existing 160 ms breath boundary after four seconds remains. It is not necessary for every caption to be a grammatically complete sentence.
- The existing eight-second normal ceiling and twelve-second ceiling for a recently unfinished clause remain bounded. Early short pauses remain protected. No extra capture timer, model pass, download or additional wait was introduced.
- Adjacent cue sample positions and utterance identity remain intact for the existing context-recovery path. Accepted finals retain their queue order; a new source does not cancel the previous caption's translation in either single-pair or four-group display mode.

This change is in the browser capture segmenter. It does not replace the desktop microphone's separate Silero input path or FFmpeg media segmentation. Both local App and extension carry version 4.5.9 for the installed browser-to-desktop workflow.

- **832 desktop tests across 87 files** pass. TypeScript, production build and lint pass; lint has the same 16 existing warnings and no errors.

This small warm-model A/B excludes browser UI and is not a general speed guarantee. Later repetitions can reuse caches.

The final output retained these details; the unfinished phrase remained `那個有點……` rather than inventing a reason. These authored controls are not an accent/noise or native-speaker fluency benchmark. Raw outputs are included for review; wording and speaker perspective can still need improvement.

## Limits

VAD pauses are evidence of a possible breath, not a guarantee of sentence meaning. Music, noisy speech, very long uninterrupted speech or a verb/negation spoken after a pause can still yield an imperfect boundary. Existing context recovery and bounded queues remain important. This patch does not guarantee YouTube-equivalent recognition/translation quality or elimination of overload on every device.

Public preview installers remain 4.4.5; source and local installation are 4.5.9. The multilingual recovery completed in 4.5.8 remains in place.
